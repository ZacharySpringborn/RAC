The Short Version

We'd love to throw your members a drink — on our dime.

We're three local businesses who want to say thank you to this community. The idea is simple: a 3–6 PM happy hour where every Raintree member gets a free drink, courtesy of us.

You host it and promote it to your members. We cover every drink, handle all the sign-ups and ticketing, and bring the promo assets. Every drink poured is a guaranteed sale for your bar that otherwise wouldn't have happened — and the tab is entirely ours.

No hard sell, no sales floor. Just a thank-you to the Raintree community, and a friendly face if anyone happens to have a question about homeownership or insurance.

The lift on your end is small.

A Conversation, Not a Demand

Let's figure out the bar together.

We know liquor licensing and health-department rules may call for one of your own team members to be the one actually pouring. We'd never want to put you out of compliance — so rather than assume, we'd love to talk through the approach that keeps everything above board and easy on your side.

Our Starting Suggestion
One Raintree team member, for the three hours.
Enough to keep licensing, safety, and service fully compliant — while we handle the drinks, the cost, the tickets, and the rest. If a different setup works better for your club, we're all ears.
